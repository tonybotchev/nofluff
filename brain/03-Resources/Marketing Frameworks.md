# Marketing Frameworks

## AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
A classic copywriting framework:
1. **Attention** - Grab their attention with a hook
2. **Interest** - Build interest with relevant info
3. **Desire** - Create desire by showing benefits
4. **Action** - Clear call to action

## PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution)
1. **Problem** - Identify the pain point
2. **Agitate** - Make the problem feel urgent
3. **Solution** - Present your solution

## StoryBrand (Donald Miller)
1. A character (your customer)
2. Has a problem
3. Meets a guide (you)
4. Who gives them a plan
5. And calls them to action
6. That helps them avoid failure
7. And ends in success

## Jobs To Be Done
People don't buy products - they hire them to do a job. Ask:
- What job is the customer hiring this for?
- What are the functional, emotional, and social dimensions?
